# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Lumen

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Discovery  
**Product Owner:** Israa Khaled

---

# 1. Product Description

Lumen is an anonymous mobile application that encourages users to engage in meaningful real-world experiences, reflect on those experiences, and anonymously exchange them with others.

The application intentionally minimizes time spent inside the product while maximizing meaningful experiences outside of it.

Unlike traditional social platforms, Lumen removes identity, popularity, and performance from the interaction, allowing people to connect solely through authentic human experiences.

---

# 2. Target Users

Lumen is intended for users who:

- Feel overwhelmed by modern social media.
- Want healthier digital habits.
- Enjoy reflection and intentional living.
- Seek authentic human connection.
- Value privacy and anonymity.

The product is designed around universal human experiences rather than specific demographics.

---

# 3. Product Goals

The first version of Lumen aims to:

- Encourage meaningful real-world experiences.
- Facilitate anonymous human connection.
- Create a safe space for honest reflection.
- Reduce unnecessary digital engagement.
- Promote empathy without social comparison.

---

# 4. Product Boundaries

The following features are intentionally excluded.

- Infinite scrolling
- Public profiles
- Followers
- Likes
- Comments
- Public statistics
- Popularity rankings
- Content recommendations
- Creator economy features

These exclusions are core product decisions rather than missing functionality.

---

# 5. Core Experience

Every complete session follows six stages.

## Receive

The user receives one meaningful invitation.

---

## Experience

The user leaves the application and lives the experience in reality.

---

## Reflect

The user documents thoughts and emotions after returning.

---

## Release

The reflection is anonymously exchanged with another participant.

---

## Illuminate

Meaningful exchanges contribute to the user's personal constellation.

---

## Return to Life

The session naturally ends without encouraging additional browsing.

---

# 6. Product Modules

## Authentication

Account creation and secure authentication while maintaining anonymous public interactions.

---

## Invitation

Responsible for presenting meaningful experiences to users.

---

## Reflection

Allows users to privately document meaningful experiences.

---

## Anonymous Exchange

Matches and exchanges reflections between anonymous users.

---

## Constellation

Visualizes meaningful impact through a growing constellation.

---

## Personal Space

Provides access to the user's personal journey, constellation, archived reflections, and settings.

---

# 7. Business Rules

The product follows the following rules.

- A user must complete a reflection before receiving another reflection.
- Reflections are permanently anonymous.
- Reflections cannot be edited after being released.
- Users cannot communicate directly.
- Public interaction does not exist.
- Every session should naturally conclude after completing the Core Experience.
- Identity should never influence content delivery.

Additional rules will be introduced as new features are added.

---

# 8. MVP Scope

## Included

- Authentication
- Invitation System
- Reflection Writing
- Anonymous Exchange
- Personal Space
- Constellation
- Settings

---

## Excluded

- Voice Reflections
- Photo Sharing
- AI-generated Reflections
- Communities
- Direct Messaging
- Replies
- Friend System
- Push Notifications (except essential)
- Social Features

---

# 9. Success Criteria

The MVP is considered successful if users can naturally complete the entire Core Experience without guidance.

Success is defined by:

- Understanding the flow intuitively.
- Completing the experience.
- Feeling emotionally connected.
- Returning because of meaning rather than habit.

The product intentionally values meaningful engagement over prolonged engagement.

---

# 10. Risks

Potential challenges include:

- Anonymous content moderation.
- Low-quality reflections.
- Matching fairness.
- Abuse of anonymity.
- Empty exchange queues.
- Maintaining meaningful interactions as the user base grows.

These challenges must be solved without compromising the product principles.

---

# 11. Open Questions

The following product decisions remain open.

- How should Invitations be generated?
- Should Invitations become personalized?
- What exactly causes a Constellation Star to illuminate?
- How should unmatched reflections be handled?
- Should Personal Space include analytics?
- What moderation strategy best balances safety and anonymity?

These questions will be resolved during future product discovery and iteration.
